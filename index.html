A corner shop that feeds the neighbourhood

We opened a few years ago with one big idea: a proper plate of curry should not cost a week's pocket money. My family helped me set up the counter, and my brother still tastes every pot before it goes out. That habit has never changed.

Today we serve office-goers from The ICON side, families from Highland Garden and late-night regulars who know we are open till 11. If your naan is cold or your curry is short on salt, tell us. We will fix it.

  • Wide menu: curries, biryani, paneer, dal and 12 kinds of bread
  • Half and full plates on most chicken dishes
  • Rotis from ₹12, so a full meal stays affordable
  • Dine in at the counter or take it away
  • Pay with any UPI app. Scan the QR at the counter
